How growth and age labels work

Growth shows the change in usage rate relative to the same topic’s recent baseline. A 3× rate is +200% growth. Bluesky compares the last 6 hours with the preceding 24 hours per hour, requiring at least 95% recorded stream coverage in both periods. Reddit discovery highlights repeated topics across communities and changes in feed position. Its Rising, Hot, and search samples do not establish complete Reddit usage-growth percentages. Mastodon compares its latest complete UTC day with the average of the preceding consecutive complete days (at least three). At least 20 observations are required in each comparison period. Topics are ordered by their largest measured source-specific percentage increase; sources and time windows remain visible in “Why this rank?”. Insufficient history or low-volume topics are marked without a growth number and appear after measured topics if space remains. Usage totals come from source-reported daily counts or distinct captured posts, not ranking points. Mastodon daily counts use UTC dates and may initially cover fewer than 14 days; its last full day is shown separately from today. Bluesky counts explicit hashtags in the past 24 hours and includes imported historical replay when available. These counts describe each source's coverage, not the entire internet. Reddit counts distinct collected posts with matching title words or phrases; comments and image text are excluded. Charts show raw uses or captured posts for the named source. Dates without history are gaps rather than zeros. Hover, tap, or use arrow keys for the date and count at each point.

Legacy means dated evidence places the topic more than six calendar months ago. New & Recent means the earliest available evidence is within six months. Without verified origin evidence, it is marked Estimated and may change to Legacy if older history is found. Age unknown means no dated evidence is available. First collected does not mean newly invented.

Source history uses dated trend-feed entries, story and post publication times, and daily hashtag usage. These dates show how far back the available evidence goes. They can precede our collection start, but a limited history does not establish a trend's first-ever use.

Bluesky contributes selected topics and live hashtags, Google contributes US search trends, Mastodon contributes tags observed by mastodon.social, Hacker News contributes technology stories, and Reddit contributes recurring title words and phrases from the monitored communities. Related phrases are grouped by exact normalized names and curated aliases; semantic grouping and image-meme recognition are not yet included.

The initial history builds as collection runs. Source links and age evidence are available in each topic row.
